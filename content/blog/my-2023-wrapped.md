This is a bit of an odd blog post, I thought it could’ve been a LinkedIn post but you can always consider it a personal spin-off to my usual editorial line.

For reference it usually goes like this:

*“Matteo’s newest rant against \[insert something in IT here\] that doesn’t work like it should”.*

Last tini-tiny rant of 2023:\
This year I’m not using any Spotify wrapped theme-d blog, because well, they laid off more than a thousand people. \
I am not boycotting them, only because they have some sort of monopoly of the music streaming market, but I’m still considering feasible alternatives that may (or may not) include the P-word 🏴‍☠️

So back to us, 2023 has come to an end. **YAYYY, finally.**\
Let’s now see what I achieved (or not) this year.

I moved to a new country, set up all the bureaucracy, joined and pivoted a startup, started blogging regularly, ended the startup (almost) gracefully, opened another company in the Netherlands, restarted freelancing, organized a KCD, contributed to KubeCon EU, grew my blog from 20 to 1200+ readers, lost 10 kg and started regularly go to the gym so I can stay fit in 2024!

I contributed to OSS and generally I coded more:

![Not 1000, but still kinda ok](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/1*QYTzAH6ydAk3g7YG0mKlMQ.png)

*Not 1000, but still kinda ok*

It’s time to take a serious look at [*my last year resolutions*](</blog/new-year-resolutions-of-a-fresh-cto/>) to see if I followed through and achieved what I expected from myself.

1. **Focus on continuous learning** ✅\
   I did dove deeper into Golang, some React, definitely Open-Tofu/Terraform and Kubernetes. I’m quite satisfied with this year’s learnings and some OSS contributions too! \
   For next year I might aim for more certifications even though I find it a bit lame they cost quite a bit. I missed the advent of coding too but I was too busy applying for jobs to prepare and then I was onboarding, it’s ok, I have next year.
1. **Build strong relationships with people in tech and my team**🟡\
   Unfortunately my startup didn’t launch the product and ended up kinda badly so I probably broke some of the relationships I created during the journey but I’m happy with the way I handled stress, getting laid off and starting over. \
   I managed to help some of my team members get a new job, even before getting mine, so I’m grateful.\
   I have quite a lot of new techies in my network and I know I can count on them, for next year I would like to keep in touch with people even if I move to the US as I plan to try.
1. **Communicate effectively with non-technical stakeholders**✅\
   I definitely upped my game on explaining deep tech ideas to businesspeople and investors. I can and will surely improve further in 2024 but I’m quite happy with the way I’m evolving this important skill.
1. **Prioritize diversity and inclusion**✅\
   My first hire has been a woman, I [*co-organized KCD Utrecht*](</blog/my-experience-as-kcd-organizer/>) with a diverse team, aiming for diversity and I feel that I opened a little bit more since I moved away from Italy, that unfortunately has a very big problem on diversity (not limited to gender gap). Another big point is that I have been selected on two program committees: KCD Utrecht and KubeCon EU, so I had the chance to focus on diversity when selecting and voting talks.\
   For next year I would love to ask more and more questions to keep my mind open and as bias-free as possible.
1. **Protect my and others well-being** 🟡\
   I almost burnout myself to death, but I am taking care of it now and even though it is a long journey I’m sure that I can recover from all the stress. I am sorry if I have been a source of stress for someone else and I will strive to become a better person both on the job and off.
1. **Embrace innovation**✅\
   Well, I went full on Platform Engineering, explored and learnt about AI more, also thanks to a family member that is studying in the field. Kept my cybersecurity game updated, mostly thanks to a bunch of good hackers I follow, some CTF playing, bug hunting included (with some nice rewards too).
1. **Improve my (spoken) English** ✅\
   I started public speaking, I interact daily with native speakers (and other expats), I feel very proud of the — always improvable — level I reached. Especially coming from Italy where English teaching is not the best. For next year I wanna read more books in English, starting with some psychology books
1. **\[Personal\] Get to play music more**❌\
   I only published a single cover and that’s super duper sad. I need to find some more time to practice, maybe learn a new instrument and definitely record songs and play live music.
1. **\[Personal\] Find a place to call home**❌\
   Not easy as it looked when I moved to northern Europe, welp, maybe the US will be easier to approach, especially when it comes to socializing and welcomeness, at least I hope so!
1. **\[Personal\] Handle my finances better** 🟡\
   I surely started tracking my networth in a thorough way but there’s still room for improvement, especially in the income field, if you know what I mean. I also decided to share publicly [**my personal finance templates**](<https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/16HO215slriMmPC2h3H4eXY4gEooUB-Ei>) which are basically an improved version of [Mr.rip’s NW sheets](<http://mr.rip/nw>).
1. **\[Bonus\] Got a new job** ✅\
   I will release a blog post talking about the new role!
1. **\[Bonus\] Traveled to new countries** ✅\
   Welp, I visited like 3–4 new countries and it was not really planned so yay! Excluding 4 States in the US in the count btw.
1. **\[Bonus\] Packed my whole life in a big check-in luggage, a hand luggage and a backpack** ✅\
   This is a big achievement, I let go of a lot of stuff and now I can move without looking back, for real!

Overall? I’m quite happy. I’m not complaining too much.

### Todo list for 2024

1. **Protect my and other people’s well being**\
   I’ll keep trying to be the best version of myself, have boundaries, don’t overwork myself to death, practice mindfulness, stop getting into negative places and being with negative people.
1. **Find a place to call home**\
   Move the f- away from Northern Europe to somewhere sunnier and warmer, both in terms of climate and people.
1. **Get to play music more**\
   Start a new music project that is not limited to vocal covers (I do need to scream from time to time).
1. **Stay fit**\
   This is easier than before, I just need to come back to the gym after these wonderful Holidays in Italy.
1. **Don’t stop learning**\
   I don’t think I would but a reminder never hurts.
1. **Succeed in my new role**\
   This is a challenge that I gladly take, as always, giving my professional 100%.
1. **Foster the relationships I built last year and grow my network** \
   I’ll keep close the nice people I met in 2023, reach out to new people, create more content, contribute to open-source and community. In short: I’ll be myself openly and see what happens.
1. **Learning more about diversity and inclusion**\
   I have a thousand questions about this and I will continue asking those, to discuss and to educate myself, because I want to become a better person.
1. **Handle my finances (way) better and earn more**\
   Now that I have a structure it’s way easier to keep my finances in place I can focus on maximizing my income and keeping the expenses fairly low. Not saying I’m gonna live a super-frugal life but I don’t need to buy more stuff in order to be happy.
1. **Find time to have fun (so I don’t get a burnout after a few months)**\
   Go to concerts, go to concerts, **GO TO \
   CONCERTS.** I can’t stay another whole year without going to metal concerts, sorry. I’ll fly to whatever city if I need to.\
   Is this the only way I have fun? Weeeeeell maybe I’ll play some videogames but that’s it, I’m a simple man.

That said, I wish all of you happy Holidays and happy new year! \
Peace out, see you in 2024. 🎁🎄
